One of the most memorable experiences of my studies was the opportunity to travel to **China**. Visiting Beijing gave me a chance to discover a completely different culture, and to put my years of Chinese language studies into practice.

![Temple of Heaven, Beijing]({{ '/assets/img/IMG_2306.jpeg' | relative_url }})
*Temple of Heaven (天坛, Tiāntán) — Beijing.*

### Why China?

Throughout my mathematics degree at Université de La Rochelle, I studied Chinese (Mandarin) as a minor across four semesters. The trip to China was a natural extension of that — going beyond the classroom and immersing myself in the language and culture firsthand.

### Beijing

Beijing is a city where ancient history and modern infrastructure coexist at every corner. The **Temple of Heaven** (天坛), built in the 15th century, is one of the most striking examples of Ming Dynasty architecture. Standing there, surrounded by centuries of history, was a moment I won't forget.

The trip reinforced my desire to explore Asia further — which is one of the reasons I'm looking forward to my exchange semester at **National Taiwan University (NTU)** in Fall 2026.
