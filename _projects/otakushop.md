## The story

OtakuShop started in 2020 as a freelance art business. Over 3 years it grew from selling hand-painted manga-inspired artworks to becoming a full digital platform integrating blockchain technology.

## Phase 1 — Physical art & commissions (2020–2022)

- Created and sold paintings inspired by Japanese manga
- Collaborated with a music studio to produce an album cover and associated NFTs
- Built a small customer base through social media

## Phase 2 — Digital platform (2022–present)

The project evolved into a full web platform with:

- **Art gallery** — full digital catalogue of artworks available to browse and purchase
- **Manga reader** — original manga content, accessible via annual subscription (€20/year) or free with NFT ownership
- **NFT integration** — users connect their crypto wallet; owning an OtakuShop NFT unlocks premium features (free manga access, exclusive custom order commissions)
- **Interactive games** — mini-games set in the manga universe
- **User accounts** — sign up, wallet connection, subscription management

## Tech stack

- **Frontend**: Jekyll + custom HTML/CSS/JavaScript
- **Blockchain**: NFT-gated access with wallet connectivity
- **Hosting**: GitHub Pages
